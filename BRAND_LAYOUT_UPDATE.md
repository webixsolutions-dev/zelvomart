# Zelvomart Brand Storefront Layout Update

This build refreshes the public storefront into a cohesive Zelvomart brand system while preserving the existing backend, admin, database, tracking, CRO and session replay functionality.

## Updated
- New premium branded header and department/search navigation.
- Rebuilt homepage hero as a split editorial/product showcase.
- Unified Zelvomart visual system: navy, cyan, white and controlled accent colours.
- Brand story/introduction section and stronger trust messaging.
- Redesigned category cards with a consistent collection/editorial look.
- Redesigned product cards with clearer stock, ratings, pricing and actions.
- Restyled deal banner, support CTA and COD journey.
- Added a branded Why Zelvomart manifesto section.
- Rebuilt shop hero/filter/result presentation.
- Restyled product detail, related product, support, cart and checkout presentation.
- Restyled About/Contact/policy/static pages and blog cards through the global brand system.
- Redesigned footer with support CTA, store positioning and cleaner information architecture.
- Mobile layout retains two product/category cards where practical and converts complex sections to stacked responsive layouts.

## Preserved
- Frontend/backend separation.
- Prisma/Supabase database logic.
- Admin panel and product management.
- Meta attribution, CRO and session replay features.
- Cart, COD checkout and order flow.
- Public API caching/CDN work.
- SEO metadata and product schema.
