# Product + Session Replay Modernization

Updated 04 October 2026.

## Session Replay
- Rebuilds the first rrweb snapshot immediately so the replay is visible before playback.
- Validates that a FullSnapshot exists and shows a clear diagnostic if an old/corrupt session is missing it.
- Adds Play, Pause, Restart, seek/progress, timer and playback-speed controls.
- Flushes FullSnapshot recording immediately for new sessions so short visits remain replayable.
- Keeps form masking/privacy behavior unchanged.

## Product Page
- Product purchase panel converted into a structured premium commerce card.
- Added Product Highlights table generated from real product/category/SKU/variant/stock data.
- Replaced quantity select with +/- controls.
- Preserved variants, bundles, cart, Buy Now, tracking, sticky actions and real reviews.
- Reworked information tabs to Overview, Why Choose Us, Specifications, Shipping & Warranty and Reviews.
- Product support CTA now uses a clean WhatsApp help card matching the supplied reference direction.
- Responsive/mobile layouts remain supported.
